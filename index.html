Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about reserving luxury ground transportation with PTT India.

Where can we hire a PTT luxury car on rent in India?

expand_more
Our luxury cars and chauffeur services are actively operational across New Delhi NCR (Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida), Mumbai, Goa, Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Jaipur, Udaipur, and Kolkata, with outstation coverage reaching every corner of India.

Can I rent a luxury chauffeur vehicle without a prior reservation?

expand_more
Yes, PTT accommodates urgent bookings subject to vehicle availability at our 24/7 central dispatch garage. Non-reserved requests are handled on a priority first-come-first-served basis. However, we strongly recommend reserving in advance for flagship models such as the Mercedes Maybach, S-Class, or Toyota Vellfire.

How is mileage and fuel calculated for outstation journeys?

expand_more
Mileage on luxury vehicles is charged garage-to-garage. The minimum standard billing limit for outstation chauffeur assignments is 250 km per calendar day. State road taxes, toll plazas, and parking are billed transparently at actual cost on the final invoice.

Do you offer one-way luxury airport and intercity transfers?

expand_more
Yes, PTT India provides seamless one-way intercity drops (e.g., Delhi to Jaipur, Mumbai to Pune, Bengaluru to Mysuru) as well as point-to-point VIP airport pickups and drops. Contact our 24/7 reservations desk at +91 11 425 88 888 for fixed one-way tariff cards.

Can I request professional women chauffeurs?

expand_more
Absolutely. PTT has a dedicated cadre of professionally trained, background-verified women chauffeurs available upon advance request for solo women business executives, families, and diplomats to ensure absolute peace of mind.
